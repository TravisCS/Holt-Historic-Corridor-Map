Holt Historic Corridor: Ontario to Pomona

Pomona draws, Ontario hosts, and one road ties them together

This map is locked to one historic corridor: Holt Boulevard, the east-west road that the West Valley Connector bus rapid transit line runs on, from Ontario International Airport and the Ontario Convention Center in the east, through downtown Ontario on Euclid Avenue and Montclair, to downtown Pomona, the Pomona Transit Center, the Pomona Arts Colony and Fairplex in the west. You can pan and zoom anywhere inside it; you cannot leave it. Use it to explore and define what a visitor partnership between the City of Ontario, the City of Pomona and Fairplex would actually contain.

Pomona drawsFairplex, the Knight Riders Cricket Ground, the LA County Fair, the Millard Sheets Art Center, the Arts Colony galleries and downtown Pomona dining.
Ontario hostsThe airport, the Convention Center and its expansion, the convention hotel, the hotel rooms along Holt, and the historic downtown on Euclid Avenue.
Transit connectsThe West Valley Connector on Holt, Metrolink and the Metro A Line at Pomona and Montclair, and the two-mile gap between the Pomona Transit Center and Fairplex.

How to read it

Hotel: color is the tier, the number is the room count, size grows with rooms. A dashed ring means a hotel outside Ontario. Pomona asset (green) and Ontario asset (red): venues, districts and civic anchors with a card each. Business along Holt: small dot colored by category; dots gather into numbered circles when zoomed out. Tap to spread. Holt spine: the corridor's centerline, Fairplex side of Pomona to Vineyard Avenue. West Valley Connector route and stations; Metrolink and Metro A Line stations are squares. The gap: the two miles between the Pomona Transit Center and Fairplex that no frequent transit covers yet.

Where the data comes from

Hotels carry over from the Ontario Hotel Map (GOCAL roster, official sites and meetings directories, October 2026). Corridor assets cite the City of Ontario, the City of Pomona, Fairplex, Major League Cricket, Metrolink, LA Metro, Omnitrans and SBCTA. Businesses along Holt come from OpenStreetMap and from official and press sources named on each card, placed by address. City limits are the Board of Equalization's official boundaries. Google ratings, review counts and photos load live when a card opens and are never stored. Every link was checked on November 1, 2026; the full list is under "About the data" in the Legend.

About the data and sources

What is shown.

  • The view is locked to a box from the west side of Fairplex to Ontario International Airport, and from the Pomona North and Montclair stations in the north to the East Ontario Metrolink station in the south, so the map can be used to define the corridor without drifting off it.
  • Hotels are the properties from the Ontario Hotel Map that fall inside the box: Ontario hotels from the GOCAL roster and the City's info sheets, and Pomona, Montclair and Claremont hotels from directory listings checked against official sites and meetings directories, with room counts and sources on each card [1] [2] [3].
  • Pomona and Ontario assets are the venues, districts, stations and civic anchors a partnership would be built around; each card names the official page it was read from.
  • Businesses along Holt are restaurants, shops and services on or within about a quarter mile of Holt between Euclid Avenue and the Pomona Transit Center: every named business tagged in OpenStreetMap in that band, plus businesses named by official and press sources, placed by address. Churches, schools and parks from OpenStreetMap are left out. The list is a sample of the corridor, not a census of it.
  • The Holt spine is the road's centerline merged from OpenStreetMap way geometry [4]. The West Valley Connector alignment and stations follow SBCTA and Omnitrans [5] [6]; rail lines are drawn from OpenStreetMap track geometry and stations from Metrolink and LA Metro [7] [8].
  • City limits are the official boundaries from the Board of Equalization layer, loaded live with a saved copy as fallback [9].
  • When a hotel, business or dining card opens, its Google rating, review count, price band and current photo are fetched live from Google Places under the map owner's key and shown with a link to the Google listing; they are not stored in this repository [10].
